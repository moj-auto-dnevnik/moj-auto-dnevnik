Everything about your vehicle in one place

A premium, fully offline vehicle logbook for Android. Track fuel, services, tires, registration, insurance and every expense — with analytics, reminders and PDF reports. Your data stays on your device.

Core features

Built for real driving life

Four pillars, tightly integrated, sharing one source of truth for every calculation.

Fuel

Log every refuel or charge. Consumption is calculated automatically from your odometer readings.

Services

Oil, brakes, filters, timing belt, technical inspection and more — with reminders based on your history.

Expenses

Registration, insurance, vignettes, fines, tolls and parking — all consolidated in your primary currency.

Analytics

KPIs, monthly stacked bars, donut breakdown and yearly reports — always consistent with your data.

Why Moj Auto Dnevnik

Built around your privacy

No accounts, no cloud, no ads. What you record stays on your device.

Private by design

All data lives locally on your device. No sign-up, no server, no analytics or tracking SDKs.

Works fully offline

Every core feature works with no internet — reminders, analytics and PDF export included.

Multi-currency

Any ISO 4217 currency, converted to your vehicle's primary currency with the rate stored per entry.

Smart reminders

Registration, insurance, vignettes, tire changes and services — calculated from your own history.

PDF & JSON export

Generate a Service Book, an Annual Report, or a full JSON backup — all produced on your device.

Six languages

English, German, Serbian (Latin & Cyrillic), Bosnian, and Croatian — switch anytime.

How it works

Set up in minutes

1

Add your vehicle

Name, make, model, engine type and primary currency. Petrol, diesel, LPG, CNG, hybrid or electric — all supported.

2

Log entries

Refuels, services, tires, registration, insurance, vignettes, and any other expense. Everything is optional.

3

See the picture

Dashboard KPIs, analytics and reminders update automatically. Export a Service Book or Annual Report as PDF.
