## 2. What the App is

Moj Auto Dnevnik is a personal vehicle logbook for Android that helps you track fuel and charging, services, tires, registration, insurance, vignettes, fines, and other vehicle-related expenses. It also provides a dashboard, analytics, reminders, and PDF/JSON exports.

The App is designed to work fully offline. All your data is stored locally on your device. The App does not use user accounts, does not sync to any server, and does not share your data with third parties.

The only network activity performed by the App is an optional refresh of official European Central Bank (ECB) daily reference exchange rates, used to convert expenses recorded in other currencies into your vehicle's primary currency. This is described in detail in the Privacy Policy.

## 7. Exchange rates

The App uses a layered approach to convert amounts entered in other currencies into your vehicle's primary currency:

1. Manual exchange rates you enter for a specific record;
2. Cached rates previously refreshed from the official European Central Bank (ECB) daily reference exchange rates;
3. Static reference rates bundled with the App.

Exchange rates are provided for **informational purposes only** and may not match the rate used by your bank, card issuer, fuel station, or other payment provider. The App does not perform any financial transaction.

Refreshing rates is optional and requires an internet connection. When you refresh, only historical entries that used the previous cached rate are updated as designed; entries with a manual exchange rate remain unchanged.
