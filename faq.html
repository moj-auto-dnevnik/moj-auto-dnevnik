Česta pitanja

Odgovori o aplikaciji, podacima, kupovini i podržanim funkcijama.

Početak

Šta je Moj Auto Dnevnik?

Privatna aplikacija za evidenciju vozila, goriva, servisa, troškova, dokumenata i rokova.

Da li mi je potreban nalog?

Ne. Nema registracije ni prijave.

Koje su trenutne verzije?

Android 1.2 je dostupan na Google Play-u. Na App Store-u je iOS 1.1, a verzija 1.2 za iPhone stiže uskoro.

Dolaze li podsetnici sami?

Da. Aplikacija vas obavesti pre isteka registracije, osiguranja ili servisa, a widget na početnom ekranu pokazuje sledeći rok.

Koje jezike aplikacija podržava?

Srpski latinica, srpski ćirilica, bosanski, hrvatski, engleski i nemački.

Vozila i evidencija

Koje tipove vozila mogu da dodam?

Automobil, motocikl, kamion, autobus, kombi i traktor.

Mogu li da pratim više vozila?

Da. Svako vozilo ima zasebnu istoriju, analitiku i rokove.

Kako se prate traktori?

Servisi i podsetnici koriste radne sate i datum umesto kilometarskih intervala.

Postoji li glasovni unos?

Da. Recite gde ste točili, koliko i po kojoj ceni — aplikacija popuni polja, vi ih pregledate i tek onda sačuvate. Ručni unos ostaje kao do sada.

Kako sinhronizujem podatke između telefona?

Preko iste Wi-Fi mreže: jedan telefon prikaže QR kod, drugi ga skenira, i izabrana vozila se prenesu za nekoliko sekundi. Bez naloga i bez interneta — podaci idu direktno sa telefona na telefon.

Troškovi i izveštaji

Koje valute su podržane?

RSD, EUR, BAM, USD i AUD, uz sačuvan kurs za svaki unos.

Mogu li videti cene goriva po pumpi?

Da. Aplikacija pokazuje cene po pumpi, poslednju, najjeftiniju i prosek za izabrani period.

Koji izvozi postoje?

PDF godišnji izveštaj, PDF servisna knjižica, CSV izvoz za Excel, JSON rezervna kopija i ICS kalendar rokova.

Da li osvežavanje kursa menja stare unose?

Ne. Istorijski unos čuva kurs primenjen prilikom čuvanja.

Privatnost i podaci

Da li aplikacija radi bez interneta?

Da. Sve osnovne funkcije rade offline. Internet je opcionalan za osvežavanje kursa.

Gde se čuvaju podaci?

Lokalno na uređaju, bez cloud sinhronizacije.

Kako prenosim podatke?

Izvezite JSON rezervnu kopiju u Podešavanjima i uvezite je na drugom uređaju.

Da li aplikacija prikazuje oglase?

Ne. Nema oglasa ni tracking SDK-ova.

Help

Frequently asked questions

Answers about the app, data, purchase and supported features.

Getting started

What is Moj Auto Dnevnik?

A private app for vehicle, fuel, service, cost, document and deadline records.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no registration or sign-in.

What are the current versions?

Android 1.2 is available on Google Play. iOS 1.1 is on the App Store, with version 1.2 for iPhone coming soon.

Do reminders come on their own?

Yes. The app notifies you before a registration, insurance or service expires, and the home-screen widget shows the next deadline.

Which languages are supported?

Serbian Latin, Serbian Cyrillic, Bosnian, Croatian, English and German.

Vehicles and records

Which vehicle types can I add?

Car, motorcycle, truck, bus, van and tractor.

Can I track multiple vehicles?

Yes. Each vehicle has its own history, analytics and deadlines.

How are tractors tracked?

Service records and reminders use operating hours and dates instead of mileage intervals.

Is there voice entry?

Yes. Say where you filled up, how much and at what price — the app fills the fields, you review them and only then save. Manual entry works just as before.

How do I sync data between phones?

Over the same Wi-Fi network: one phone shows a QR code, the other scans it, and selected vehicles transfer in seconds. No account and no internet — data goes straight from phone to phone.

Costs and reports

Which currencies are supported?

RSD, EUR, BAM, USD and AUD, with the applied rate saved on every entry.

Can I see fuel prices per station?

Yes. The app shows prices per station, the latest, the cheapest and the average for the chosen period.

Which exports are available?

PDF annual report, PDF service book, CSV export for Excel, JSON backup and ICS deadline calendar.

Does refreshing rates change old records?

No. Historical records keep the rate applied when they were saved.

Privacy and data

Does the app work offline?

Yes. All core features work offline. Internet is optional for exchange-rate refreshes.

Where is my data stored?

Locally on your device, without cloud synchronization.

How do I move my data?

Export a JSON backup in Settings and import it on the other device.

Does the app show ads?

No. There are no ads or tracking SDKs.
